About us

Coffee that keeps up with you.

Better Coffee started with a simple frustration: canned coffee was either loaded with sugar or tasted like an afterthought. We wanted one we’d actually reach for every day.

So we built it from the coffee up: 100% Arabica, cold brewed for a smooth, low-acid cup, made with oat milk and 0g sugar. Then we gave each part of the day its own can: Focus, Calm and Energy.
